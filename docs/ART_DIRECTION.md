# Living campus art direction

The first supplied reference informs the organized campus plan: pale masonry blocks, repeated recessed glazing, shaded pedestrian courts, tree-lined avenues, rooftop infrastructure, and a strategic oblique camera. Its dashboard density is reduced so the campus occupies most of the game view.

The second reference informs only restrained warm/cool lighting contrast and a crisp interface accent. Its enclosed science-fiction corridor is not the campus architecture target.

## Representative block checkpoint

Build an academic courtyard with three connected wings, four floors, dark recessed window bays, warm stone vertical fins, roof parapets and plant, a glass entrance canopy, paved approach, planted islands, mature trees, benches, lamps and walking students. Repetition establishes scale; alternate façade depths and multiple volumes avoid a single primitive block appearance.

Use authored procedural assemblies with material-group mesh combination for static details. Retain simple collision volumes at facility level. Avoid thousands of independent window renderers. Use opaque reflective dark glazing in the strategic view, not expensive overlapping transparency. Street signage and cyan selection footprints should remain readable at overview scale.

Palette: warm off-white stone, charcoal metal, cool blue glazing, muted green landscape, pale concrete walks, dark asphalt, and limited cyan operational accents. Lighting: late morning soft sunlight with cool ambient fill, restrained bloom and ACES tonemapping.

## Review criteria

Capture the actual running Unity player at 1920×1080, with overview and academic focus views. Check architectural depth, scale, path continuity, ground contact, shadow readability, selection visibility and HUD occlusion. The supplied reference is a quality direction, not evidence that our render matches it. Record shortcomings before expanding the campus.

A twelve-second uncapped frame-time sample is an early diagnostic only, not proof of sustained 60 FPS. Full-scene testing and a longer target-hardware run remain separate checks.
